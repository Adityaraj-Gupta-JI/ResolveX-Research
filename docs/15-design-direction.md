# Visual direction — ResolveX

The supplied reference posters are inspiration for **mood and graphic language only**. Do not reproduce their artwork, typography, compositions, logos, or recognizable characters.

## Concept: Acid Editorial × Research Instrument
Combine expressive editorial typography, playful abstract anthropomorphic micro-illustrations, hand-drawn graffiti-like strokes, and disciplined scientific data panels. The contrast is deliberate: playful entry points, precise computation and proof views.

## Visual system
- Two modes: `Editorial` landing/learning and `Instrument` proof/benchmark workspace.
- Strong typography hierarchy; do not rely on any specific licensed font.
- Abstract mascot: an original modular `X`-shaped figure composed of geometric shapes, not copied from poster characters.
- Scientific plots: perceptually uniform, colorblind-conscious continuous scales with legends, units, axis labels, and data provenance.
- Graffiti-inspired expressive lines only as nonessential decoration; never obscure proofs, equations, or navigation.
- Respect reduced motion, keyboard navigation, and small screens.

## Screens
1. Home / research manifesto.
2. Problem selection: PCP or termination.
3. Input editor and run configuration.
4. State explorer and trace playback.
5. Proof inspector with verified/unknown distinction.
6. Benchmark laboratory with heatmaps, contours, polar charts, 3D surfaces.
7. Research notebook with hypothesis and experiment traceability.

## Originality gate
Before release, compare new artwork against references to avoid close resemblance. Do not embed the user-supplied reference posters in the public repository without rights/permission.
